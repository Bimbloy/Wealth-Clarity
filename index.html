📸 Scan a Receipt ▸
Take or choose a photo of a receipt. We'll split it into individual items — pizza to Food, a jump pass to Entertainment, socks to Shopping — instead of lumping the whole thing under one merchant.
🔒 Your photo is processed entirely on your device using on-device text recognition. It is never uploaded, sent, or stored anywhere.
📋 Works best on simple, single-column receipts (grocery, retail, restaurant). Multi-column tables — like event bookings with separate Qty/Price/Subtotal columns — are harder to read accurately. We'll flag it below if something looks off, but it's worth a manual check either way on receipts like that.
💡 Tip: flatten each receipt, use good lighting, and fill the frame for the most accurate results.
📱 On a phone: to scan more than one at once, choose Photo Library (not the camera) when prompted, then tap to select several. The camera only captures one at a time.

            📖 How to Use This App
            ▸
          

          
Wealth Clarity works right in your browser — there's nothing to download from an app store. For quick access, you can save it to your phone's home screen so it opens like a regular app.


          

            
📱 Save to Home Screen — iPhone / iPad (Safari)

            

              1. Open this page in Safari (not Chrome — "Add to Home Screen" only works in Safari on iPhone).

              2. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow pointing up, at the bottom of the screen).

              3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.

              4. Tap Add in the top corner. The Wealth Clarity icon will appear on your home screen.
            

          


          

            
🤖 Save to Home Screen — Android (Chrome)

            

              1. Open this page in Chrome.

              2. Tap the ⋮ menu (three dots, top-right).

              3. Tap Add to Home screen (or Install app, if shown).

              4. Tap Add to confirm. The icon will appear on your home screen.
            

          


          

            
💻 Using it on a computer

            
Just keep this page open, or bookmark it (Ctrl+D on Windows, ⌘+D on Mac) so you can return to it anytime. All your data stays saved in that browser on that device.

          


          

            
⚠️ Good to know

            
Your data is saved on the specific device and browser you use. If you switch phones or browsers, use Backup & Restore (above) to move your data across. And because everything is stored locally, clearing your browser data will also clear your entries — so keep a backup.

          

        


        
        

          
            🔒 Data & Privacy
            ▸
          

          

            
Where is my data?

            
All your data is stored locally on this device only. We don't have servers, databases, or cloud storage. Too cool right?

          


          

            
What if I change devices?

            
Export your data as CSV (see above), keep it safe, and import it on your new device when you use Wealth Clarity.

          


          

            
Can you see my data?

            
No. We cannot and do not collect any of your information. Your financial data never leaves your device.

          


          

            
What about statements I upload for import?

            
PDF statements are read and parsed entirely inside your browser using an open-source PDF reader. The file is never uploaded to a server — it's processed in memory on your device and discarded once you close the tab. You review and approve every transaction before anything is saved.

          


          

            
What about receipt photos I scan?

            
Receipt photos are read using on-device text recognition (Tesseract.js) that runs in your browser via WebAssembly. The image is never uploaded anywhere. We try to split the receipt into individual items and categorize each one (pizza → Food, a jump pass → Entertainment) rather than lumping everything under one merchant — accuracy varies by receipt layout, so always review before importing.
